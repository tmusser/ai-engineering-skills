# Gotchas

_Optional durable sharp edges that are easy to miss and likely to recur. Do not create this file for ordinary bugs, temporary failures, or generic reminders._

## G1 - _Short name_

- Trigger: _When does this matter?_
- Gotcha: _What non-obvious behavior or constraint surprises people?_
- Consequence: _What breaks or becomes unsafe if it is ignored?_
- Safe path: _What should the next agent or human do instead?_
- Evidence: _Command, source file, issue, test, or other grounding._
- Last verified: _YYYY-MM-DD or commit_
- Status: active | resolved
